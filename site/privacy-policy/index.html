Privacy Policy

Utilization of the Data Collected

The only purpose of the personal data gathered by GlobalMarco is to enhance our website, your experience, customer service, handle purchases and transactions, communicate, and receive emails.

When necessary for fraud prevention or investigation, GlobalMarco may share information with governmental organisations or other law enforcement organisations. Information is not given to businesses for marketing or advertising purposes. Information is not shared, exchanged, or sold to outside parties; it is used exclusively by GlobalMarco.

The Privacy Policy only applies to GlobalMarco. Links to other websites are available on our website. The conduct or principles of such third parties and other websites are not the responsibility of GlobalMarco. Please read the privacy statement for every other website you visit.


Collection Of Information

Names, email addresses, and other private information are gathered by GlobalMarco from clients and site users. Only when a client or visitor fills out one of our online forms is this information gathered. These forms may be a quotation form, an order form, or a mailing list registration form, among others. Not shared, exchanged, or sold to outside parties, this data is utilised only by GlobalMarco.

GlobalMarco makes use of tracking technologies like cookies. These are simply used to collect information on how to make our website better, how many people visit it, how they use it, and to keep track of the things in their shopping carts. Additionally, cookies can be used to tailor a website for recurring visitors. Cookies cannot be used to acquire personal information.

Except as required by law, GlobalMarco does not intentionally collect or solicit Personally Identifiable Information from or about children under the age of 13. We will immediately remove any information we find that was provided by a child under 13 in violation of our policy. Please get in touch with us at the address provided below if you think GlobalMarco may have any information from or about anybody under the age of 13.


Data Security

It's crucial for GlobalMarco to protect customer information. Client information is only accessible to highly trained staff.

Secure Sockets Layer, or SSL, is used to safeguard servers. To execute online purchases securely, including the processing of credit card data, we employ SSL. Other individuals find it tough to intercept and read because to the security safeguards. This is handled for us by SSL, which employs a mix of software and encryption and decryption.

Unless the owner of the credit card gives permission to set up an account, credit card information is not kept on file.

All users of these services must be 18 years of age or older in order to register with them and use them. These services are not designed for use by children.
